01

Who we are

ShelfZero is a household pantry, meal-planning, and shopping-list app built to help you track what food you have and use it before it goes to waste. This policy covers the ShelfZero mobile app and the backend service it talks to.

02

Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to actually function. Here's everything, in one place:

CategoryWhat's included
AccountYour email address and password, used to sign in.
ProfileName, location (city/state you type in — we never access your device's precise location), household size, cuisine preferences, dietary preferences, allergies, and goals, all collected during setup.
Pantry & shopping dataThe food items you add — names, quantities, units, categories, and expiry dates — plus your shopping list.
Household & invitesNames and roles of people in your household, and the email or phone number you enter when inviting someone to join.
Meal plansMeal suggestions generated from your current pantry contents and your stated preferences.

We don't use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in this app — there's nothing in ShelfZero collecting behavioral data about how you use it beyond what's listed above.

03

Camera & scanning

ShelfZero asks for camera access so you can scan receipts and barcodes to add pantry items automatically. When you take a photo to scan, it's sent to our backend to identify items and pull them into your pantry — the camera is never accessed in the background, and photos are only captured when you actively use the Scan screen.

04

How we use it

  • To run the core features: your pantry list, shopping list, meal plans, and household sharing.
  • To personalize meal suggestions using your pantry contents and preferences.
  • To send you local reminders on your own device — like an item expiring soon, or someone accepting your invite — if you've turned notifications on.
  • To let household members you've invited see and edit shared pantry and shopping-list data.
05

Where it's stored

Pantry items, shopping lists, household membership, and invites are stored on our backend server so they can sync across your household's devices. Your onboarding profile, app appearance setting, and donation history are currently stored only on your own device, not on our servers. Notification reminders are scheduled locally on your device — we don't operate a push-notification server, so nothing about your expiry reminders is sent anywhere.

06

Sharing with others

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. The only "sharing" that happens is the sharing you set up yourself:

  • Anyone you invite into your household can see and edit the pantry and shopping list you share with them.
  • The contact info (email or phone) you enter to invite someone is used only to deliver that invite.
07

Your choices

  • You can edit your name, location, household size, and dietary/cuisine preferences any time from your Profile.
  • You can turn notification reminders on or off from the Household screen.
  • You can remove a household member, or sign out, at any time.
  • Full in-app account and data deletion is on our roadmap and not yet available. Until then, you can request that your account and data be deleted by contacting us at the address below.
08

Children's privacy

ShelfZero is meant for adults managing a household and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.
